Build a modern, high-converting, responsive single-page website for "Albrecht Works" (domain: albrechtworks.com), an independent business operations, technology, and workflow automation consultancy based in Libertyville, Illinois, founded by Chris Albrecht.

### 1. Technology & Design Framework
- Platform: Clean, responsive single-page web application using HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.
- Typography: Use Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and Inter for body text (clean, legible, modern).
- Color Palette:
  - Background: Clean White (#FFFFFF) with subtle Off-White/Canvas sections (#F8FAFC)
  - Primary Text & Headings: Deep Slate Navy (#0F172A)
  - Secondary Text: Neutral Slate (#475569)
  - Accent / Primary CTA: Royal Blue (#2563EB), Hover: (#1D4ED8)
  - Borders: Light Slate (#E2E8F0)
- Aesthetic: Mature, restrained, and trustworthy. NO generic AI brains, neon graphics, robotic artwork, or cheesy tech stock photos. Focus on clean white cards, generous whitespace, crisp typography, and 1px borders.
